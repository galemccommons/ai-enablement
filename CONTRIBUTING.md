# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve this repository. You do not need to be an engineer to suggest a change.

## Propose a change

Open an issue to share an idea, report a problem, or ask a question. Describe what you would like to change, why it matters, and any examples that may help the team understand it. Use an existing issue template when one fits.

## Submit a pull request

If you or someone on your team can make the change, open a pull request (PR) linked to the related issue. Briefly explain what changed and include any context a reviewer will need. We welcome small, focused PRs and may ask follow-up questions before merging.

## Response time

We aim to acknowledge new issues and pull requests within five business days. More complex requests may take longer to review, but we will share an update when possible.

**Code of Conduct:** See the repository's future `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` for participation expectations.
